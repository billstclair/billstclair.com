Wolfe's Lodge Mirror

This directory contains a mirror of Claire Wolfe's Lodge as of 12/17/2001. The bits came from this link at archive.org, the last date they sampled before the Lodge disappeared from curleywolfe.net. Thank you Brewster!

A few of the files were missing from the archive, but most of them are here. Enjoy!

ClaireWolfe.com is Claire's current home on the web, including Wolfesblog, her weblog.

The Claire Files is an archive of Claire's writings maintained by Debra Ricketts.

This page at LewRockwell.com links to some more recent essays by Patricia Neill.
