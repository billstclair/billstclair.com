The Six Blind Men and the Elephant

The WIZ project consists of quite a few separate parts. A micro-chip whose design is a departure from current design trends. A non-network whose design is nothing like existing networks. A notion of programming languages that changes the notion of what languages are. An emulation of the mind unlike any other heretofore. A number of new paradymes that will transform the nature of society's relationship with all forms of mechanical, electronic, and communication technologies.

Telling the story of the WIZ is like the problem of the Six Blind Men and the Elephant: Six blind men want to find out what an elephant is. Is it a plant? A mineral? A vegetable? They are taken to a zoo and led to a giant elephant. One of them grabs hold of the tail, and says, "Now I know what an elephant is: It is a kind of rope." Another stands next to a leg and says, "An elephant is a tall cylindrical column". The third grabs the trunk and says "An elephant is like a snake." The fourth grabs an ear and says "An elephant is thin and soft and floppy." I forget what the 5th and 6th say. You get the point.

Which is, that no matter how well I describe and you understand any one aspect of the WIZ project, you cannot fully appreciate any part without knowing the whole. The WIZ consists of a number of highly interdependent parts. These include:

  • A new chip, the WIZ microprocessor chip.
  • A new notion of how memory works
  • A new kind of non-network.
  • A new notion of programming languages.
  • A new model of the mind in software.
  • A new paradyme for the way humans relate to mechanical objects and the physical world.
  • A new paradyme for communication between humans.
None of these pieces stands alone. Each cannot be fully understood except in relation to the whole project. Thus, the WIZ is the total project, not any one piece. It is a vision of a new future.

But we must start somewhere.....
