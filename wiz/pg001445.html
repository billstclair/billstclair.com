The WIZ Central Memory Bank

A WIZ has no memory as we generally think of computer memory. It has no local storage -- no RAM, no floppy disks, no hard disks, no tapes, no files. It just has a single storage paradyme -- all WIZ data and programs are stored as "memories" in the "Central Memory Bank".

Thus, "Steve Bush's favorite color" is a memory which might contain "BLUE". It is not stored at or in any particular WIZ -- it is stored in the Central Memory Bank. Indeed, no WIZ has its "own" memories, all WIZ memories are in the Central Memory Bank, which is shared by ALL WIZes.

And when I say ALL I mean ALL!  Consider it a single giant memory bank located in the center of the universe, with all WIZes connected directly to it. When a WIZ stores a data item into "its" memory, it stores it into the central memory bank, which is also the memory of all other WIZes.

The central memory bank is conceptually located nowhere and located everywhere. This might be compared to the idea of where your money "is" when you put it into your bank account. Say you walk into the Los Angeles branch of your bank and deposit ten dollars into your account. Then you fly to Munich and deposit another ten dollars into your account. Then you fly to Durban South Africa and ask the teller there how much was put into your account today, and he tells you twenty dollars. And you withdraw fifteen of it. So where is your money? You put it in at one place, and take it out at another! It is nowhere and it is everywhere.

True, the exact physical bills you deposited are stored somewhere. But they become almost irrelevant. An electronic copy of your money is put onto an electronic network in such a way that the "money" is conceptually everywhere at once. You can walk into any bank in the world and withdraw it. Not the same physical bills, of course, but close enough for all practical purposes!

Likewise, any data item stored by a WIZ into "its" memory is stored into the memory of all WIZes!

This makes programming a WIZ very simple. If a WIZ wanted to know "Steve Bush's favorite color" it could just look into its memory and the answer would be there. There is no concept of where that data item physically is. It is in the Central Memory.

When a WIZ needs to look at a data item, it just does, and there it is! There is no concept of a network, no concept of having to "transmit" or "receive" the data from somewhere "else". No "file" to "open", no "comm line" to "initialize". There is just its memory, the Central Memory.
