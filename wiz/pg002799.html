Introduction to WIZ-objects

I have a view of "robot" that is very very non-anthropomorphic. I envision that just about every single OBJECT of a mechanical or electrical nature will become intelligent.

Start with a single chip, about the size of a pea, with enough internal power to last for years, a low power radio link to other chips like itself, and a WIZ processor. And give it a few simple sensors, digital vision, audio mike, inertial and magnetic position sense, ability to sense many kinds of chemicals (smells), etc, etc, etc. Not all of them would have all kinds of sensors. And assume it costs about a dime to mass manufacture. This is the "WIZ" chip.

Now put a WIZ into every mechanical or electrical object you can think of. A light bulb. A windshield wiper. A shoe. This WIZ will act as a "brain" in all mechanical or electrical objects. It will impart a bit of "intelligence" to that object.

"Shoe, how far have I walked today?". "Light, from now on just come on whenever anyone walks in the room, ok. If I don't want you on, I'll tell you, ok? Thanks!". And "windshield wiper, listen to the radio and beat in time with the music, will 'ya."

If a cigarette lighter had a brain, what could it do? Light itself when picked up and placed within range of a cigarette? Shut off when it detected the cigarette was lit? Put out enough extra flame to compensate for the wind, if any? How about refusing to light after you've had your quota of cigarette for the day! How about, "candle mode" so you can set it down and light a darkened room, with auto shutoff if it falls over or gets forgotten about. And always shutoff if anything other than a cigarette gets near the flame (unless commanded otherwize).

A robot in the shape of a human is all wrong. I foresee a "race" of little "creatures", shaped like cigarette lighters, windshield wipers, disc brakes, shoes, light bulbs, etc -- because they ARE cigarette lighters, windshield wipers, disc brakes, shoes, light bulbs, etc.
