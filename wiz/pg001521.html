A silent technology that isn't there.

The vast majority of WIZes will be unheard of and un-thought of, existing hidden in the simple objects around us, which will simply become more natural and more intelligent, but less obvious or needing attention.

Science fiction writer Arthur C. Clarke wrote that "Any technology that is sufficiently advanced will appear to be magic". The technology I seek is a technology that actually disappears from consciousness. Simplicity is the key.

A WIZ-light bulb just lights up when it should. Like when someone walks into the room.

How would a WIZ-door unlock itself? In sci-fi movies, the guy walks up to it, and a cold computer voice says "Please identify yourself!". Then he speaks his name and password. And there's this ominous little "eye" looking at him. You are completely aware that a computer is watching - running the show - and maybe it will let him in, and maybe it won't. Finally the voice says, "Access permitted" and the door loudly clicks open. Pretty scary, huh?

Why not have no interaction whatsoever? You walk up to the door, you put your hand on the knob, you turn it, and you open the door.

If it was locked, it simply wouldn't open! What could be more simple? THIS IS EXACTLY HOW DOORS WORK NOW! How do you tell if a door is locked? You put your hand on the knob and try to open it. If it won't open, the door is locked. Like, duh!

The WIZ-door is being very intelligent. If it looks at you, the camera is unobtrusive. Maybe it analyses a sample of your breath through a hidden sensor and checks your DNA. Maybe it scans your fingerprints as they touch the doorknob. It doesn't really matter how, but somehow it determines who you are. It does this silently and unobtrusively.

If a computer can scan fingerprints, couldn't it just as well get them from your fingers as they grabbed the doorknob? Why do you have to lay your hand flat on a translucent white scanner surface and leave it there motionless for several seconds? In the movies, why do they have the guy stick his eye up to a "retinal scanner" device? If it can scan his eye from 1/2 inch away, couldn't it scan it from 6 feet away?

I mean, is it our job to make things easier for the computer, or the computer's job to make things easier for us? Come on!

The point is, technology must become INVISIBLE. The end result is a door that works like a door: you grab the doorknob and pull. The room lights come on. Things just work like you expect them to.

We seem to have a fascination with fiddling knobs and dials. My washing machine has 5 separate dials to set for every washload. The "digital" version is even more complex! And let's not even think about the average VCR remote control these days!

Now notice also that I started out by saying, "How would a WIZ-door unlock itself?". I didn't say "How would a WIZ unlock a door?", I said "How would a "WIZ-door unlock itself." Thus, the door is no longer just a lump of matter. It has energy. It thinks, it acts, it communicates. In the Sci-Fi movie, the computer is separate from the door, and the computer controls the door. In my vision, the door is the door, and it isn't controlled by a WIZ, it IS a WIZ.

And while the door is basically silent, you could actually talk to it, if you wanted to. Perhaps to ask "Who has gone into this room today?" or "Door, is Mary in there?", or even "Door, why won't you let me in?". But mostly, you would just grab the knob and walk in!

So the idea of a WIZ-object, a physical or mechanical object with a WIZ in it, is to be intelligent enough to operate independently, and kind of "disappear into the woodwork". All little mechanical things will become like a race of beings, kind of mechanically inclined little guys which act like you'd expect them to. They will manipulate the material world for us. There will be no more switches! No more knobs, no more dials, no more things to fiddle with. The world around us will just work right, and in full accordance with our commands.

Science fiction has produced stories of robots in the shape of humans, duplicating human functions. The WIZ-object is the true ROBOT of the future.
