What is the WIZ project? Opening statements.

The WIZ is my vision of the future. It starts with the creation of a new race of creatures, little guys I call WIZes. And it follows through with how they will change the way the Human race relates to the material universe and to each other.

Physically, a WIZ is a small piece of electronics, perhaps the size of a pea. It is self-powered, and in radio communication with all other WIZes.

Electrically, a WIZ is a general purpose electronic circuit which will replace just about every other electronic circuit on the planet. Look at anything that has electronics in it today. A single pea-sized WIZ replaces it.

And functionally, a WIZ is a bit of a "mind". A WIZ-mind is a communication and control system between a human and a WIZ-object. A WIZ-mind is able to sense its environment, make recordings of its environment, compare the current environment to previously recorded environments, and make decisions which produce the best chance of success.

I see the WIZ as operating mainly in two spheres of existence. One, as a mind in all material, electrical, or mechanical objects. And secondly, as the pure beingness of a "communicator".

As the mind in a material object, a WIZ is part of an object, imbuing an intelligence to that object. Take for instance, a car's windshield wiper. It doesn't need much of a mind -  just enough to figure out when it's raining, and to look and see if it's wiping fast enough to keep the windshield clear. And, to start, change, or stop when commanded to. And in MY car, to beat in time to the music I'm listening to. And of course, it knows lots of little details like when it was installed, who installed it, where it was purchased, how many rain storms its been through, etc. After all, it was there.

Thus, the WIZ-windshield wiper, the WIZ-steering wheel, the WIZ-light bulb, the WIZ-faucet, the WIZ-toaster, etc, all become members of a "race" of little "creatures", little guys you can talk to, interact with, and who will do their jobs efficiently and intelligently.

In the second sphere of operation, a WIZ may also serve as a pure "communicator".  You can talk to it. It can serve you as a personal secretary. It can follow you around and know everything you know. Or not. It can analyze the world around it, by itself, and via other WIZes, and help you analyze the world around you. And through it, you can command any and all other WIZ-objects, thus taking complete control of the physical environment.
