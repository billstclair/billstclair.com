Introduction to the Comms

I'd like to introduce a program called the Communicator program, which creates a "creature" I'll call a "Comm". A comm is a computer projected image of a human which can interact with real humans. Comms can move around, see, hear, talk, and act. As they have no physical beingness, like ghosts, they cannot lift or move objects. A Comm's main purpose is to communicate with humans and serve as a communication system between humans.

The program operates through suitably equipped Wizes, having:

  • A 3-D holographic camera.
  • A 3-D holographic projector.
  • An audio microphone.
  • An audio speaker.
For the purposes of this discussion, I will assume an ideal manifestation of this equipment, about the size of a half dollar, and worn on the belt buckle or wrist, so that comms can project themselves and interact with us at any time and place.

Comms may be distinguishable from humans, as they will generally appear somewhat translucent or ghost-like. While the Comm hardware can display any image, I will limit the term "comm" to mean a human-like projection.

Thus, we will have a new race of beings, "comms", which will live among us.

Of course, the current (year 2000) state of the art in computer technology does not include 3-D holographic capabilities, let alone miniaturized to the size of a wrist watch! What we have is 2-D digital cameras and big bulky computer screens. I don't believe this limitation really makes must difference to the following discussion. I am interested in what can be done with a Comm, conceptually, and the quality of the image is hardly relevant in the contexts I will follow.
