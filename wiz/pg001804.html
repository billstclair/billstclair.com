The Nature of the link between WIZes

A WIZ chip is about the size of a pea, is completely self-contained, self powered, and linked to all other WIZes, often via radio.

The nature of the WIZ technology used to communicate among WIZ-objects is critical to understanding how WIZ-objects will coordinate and cooperate among themselves. As this is a technical subject, I cannot give all the details here -- this chapter was meant for non-technical readers.
