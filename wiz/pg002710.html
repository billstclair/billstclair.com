Scenarios of interaction with Comms

The Comm in mode 1 - completely independent mode - is in the realm of "artificial intelligence". Whether such a thing can be done or not, I won't say here. I want to focus on the last three modes, which require very little advancement in software or hardware concepts. In fact, all that is needed (minimally) is a telephone and a bit of advancement in voice recognition...


I want to speak (live) with my friend Joe. I say "HEY JOE!". An image of Joe appears. The Comm-projector does a seamless "fade-in" from reality by having Joe's image appear way off in the distance and hazy so that I hardly notice it at first. Then it appears to walk toward me as the image sharpens, and finally appears to be standing right in front of me. It's like Joe was always there, just off in the distance, and as I call out to him, he comes toward me.

Or, the image of Joe could just "pop up" suddenly right in front of me. Exactly how people of the future will prefer to have their Comms approach them I can't predict, but I can indicate the range of possibilities. In an audio-only system, the Comm would just start talking, there is no image to fade in or out.

So there's Joe, only it's not Joe, it's a Comm which Joe has specifically instructed to appear in his stead. Kind of like his personal secretary. It's his personal Comm. Operating in mode 2 - imitating a human mode - I talk to Joe's Comm, which looks, sounds, and acts almost exactly like Joe. I tell it I need to speak to the real Joe. It says "Just a second...". After a few seconds, the image seamlessly morphs from the fake image to the real image of the real Joe standing there. What I see is still a Comm, now operating in mode 3 - projecting a live image of a live person in real-time. The transition is seamless and hardly noticible, except perhaps Joe looks more "lifelike" now (although still being projected through a Comm and therefore still slightly translucent and unable to shake my hand!).

Joe likewise has a Comm at his location projecting my own live image through my Comm. Both our Comms are now in "Live projection mode". We talk to each other as if we were both there.

In an audio-only system, this would basically be a telephone, except that a smart program with a synthetic voice (similar to Joe's) first answers like an automatic secretary.

One of the most interesting applications of the Comms occurs by switching from mode 2 - imitating a human mode, to mode 4 - playing a recording of a human. By searching a database of suitable recordings, we have a sort of knowledge repository interface.

For instance, Mike Smith is "Mr. Science". He publishes a site where people ask questions like "Why is the sky blue?" and he answers them.

Now suppose Mike were to record all these answers with his Comm. Now suppose when you call Mike ("Hey, Mr. Science!"), his Comm approaches, introduces itself, holds a bit of conversation with you, and lets you ask any question. After you ask the question, the Comm fades (seamlessly of course) from mode 2 to mode 4, and plays you the recording of him answering the question. If the mode 1 operation is intelligent ("slick") enough, and the fade is seamless enough, you really get the feeling you just called Mike and spoke with him, asked a question, and got an answer.

Of course, if you ask a question not in the database, the Comm handles you by saying something like "I'll have to get back to you later on that one. Is there any other question you want to ask?". The unanswered question is of course played to the real Mike Smith (via a Comm in mode 4 replaying YOU asking the question), who may then answer the question. His Comm records that answer, adds it to the database, and calls you back, acting in mode 1 like Mike and telling you he has that answer you were looking for earlier.

Now, let's do an example that's a bit more interesting.

Bill Jones is a master mechanic. He wants to help people tune up some particular model of car. He gets about half a dozen friends to come and watch. He instructs them to interrupt him frequently and ask as many questions as they can, getting him to explain everything. And his Comm watches (ie, records) the entire thing. He spends all day doing what would normally take just a half-hour, because he keeps stopping to explain what he is doing, or he stops to tell a funny story about some part, or he gets interrupted for a question.

For instance, he's saying "Next you unscrew the thingamajig here." An onlooker interrupts: "What's that funny looking screwdriver you are using?". He replies, "Oh, yea, this is one of these Wizenhofer brand screwdrivers - works really well for this particular thimgamajig." Another watcher says, "Where did you get it?". "Well, I got this one at Jack's Hardware, but I think the major chains all carry it. Make sure to ask for the Wizenhofer "B" model, it's much better." And someone else interrupts to ask "Where is Jack's Hardware?", etc.

And so it goes, all day long. Now, sometime months later, I say "Hey Bill Jones", and his Comm shows up to talk to me (mode 2 - imitating Bill). I say, "Bill, can you show me how to tune up my car?". And his Comm searches its database for that question, finds the above mentioned record, switches to mode 4 and begins to replay the image of Bill Jones showing how me how to tune up my car, like he was right there. The Comm is smart enough to "splice out" all the interruptions, questions, etc, so that it takes just half an hour.

Now, in the middle, I interrupt him to ask a question. His Comm searches its database, finds where a student actually asked him almost exactly the same question, and he begins answering, just like he was right there!

So now, once Bill starts demonstrating, we are in mode 4 and stay in it, yet in the background the Comm is still "splicing" in and out various parts. When I ask a question, the Comm searches the database and splices in the answers, but we are still mode 4 to mode 4. We don't go back to mode 2 until Bill is completely finished.

I feel like I am talking with Bill. He responds to my questions, and helps me learn what I need to know.

Ralph Jones is a famous football player who has just won a big game. I say "Ralph Jones, please come here!", and his Comm appears. It looks just like him, it's so real I could almost shake his hand. It says to me: "Hi Steve, how can I help you?".

So I say, "I'm a reporter and I want to ask you some questions." He says, "Sure, go ahead" And I say, "Ralph, how'd you get past Joe Nevada there in that last touchdown play, it looked like he had you for a minute there?" He pauses for a moment. Now Ralph's Communicator doesn't know how to answer that question, so he calls the real Ralph, and Ralph's Comm seamlessly fades from simulated Ralph to real Ralph. Real Ralph answers the question. I hardly notice any change. I ask him dozens more questions, about the game, about his life, his younger days, his parents, his future plans, etc. I thank him and say good-bye.

Now Ralph says to his Comm, who is now projected standing in front of him and appearing like a beautiful secretary to him, "Susan," (that's what he call's her), "that was a good interview and I liked my answers, so I give you permission to duplicate them in my behalf anytime in the future, OK?" And she says "Got it, thanks!".

Later, someone else says "Ralph Jones, come here!", and Ralph's Comm appears. The guy asks him about that last touchdown play with Joe Nevada, and instantly Ralph's Comm (seamlessly) fades from looking like Ralph (mode 2 - imitating a human) into the recording of Ralph himself answering just that question (mode 4 - recorded projection). The guy also asks several more questions, all questions that Ralph has been asked before and that his Comm has recorded the answer to, so the guy gets his answers which all appear to come straight from Ralph.

Ralph's Comm thus seamlessly alternates from mode 2 - imitating Ralph, intercepting questions, understanding them, checking the database, and morphing to mode 4 - projecting just the relevant recording of real Ralph.

For me, this is the main purpose of the Comms. My Comm communicates for me. It alternates between a very intelligent data base interface with access to my entire recorded knowledge, and that recorded knowledge itself, straight from the source, me.

The Comm thus replaces the Book with a direct conversation with the author. We return to a time of Storytelling instead of book reading, where human interaction was more direct! While the technology is far advanced, the net result is a simpler communication - one-on-one human to human (via Comms) communication. Of course, you can always request a printed copy too!

Ralph and his Comm Susan are walking along through a garden. Well, Ralph is walking, Susan is merely projected. Ralph says, "What time is it?" and Susan answers the question. Her voice is synthesized and her image is computer generated. She is not just replaying a tape of somebody answering that question, she is creating a new communication. "Susan, see that tree over there? What kind of tree is it?". Susan checks the data bases, says, "Judging from the leaf shape, I'd say it was an Oak.". Ralph says, "How old do you think it is?", and Susan answers "I'd guess about 85 years.".

Ralph and Susan are now having a true conversation. Susan embodies (pun intended) all the knowledge and capability of the computer. "She" has access to every database there is. She has access to every algorithm (program) that can be executed. She is the "OS", the Operating System. One can ask her any question that one can ask a computer. Because, after all, she is a computer.

Thus, the Comms, are now two things. They are communication vias between humans, vias which transcend time and space (via recordings made earlier and elsewhere). But, especially in mode 1, a Comm is also the "human interface" to the computer of the future.

For instance, Ralph has an accountant named "Fred". Fred is a Comm. He never duplicates any real person - there is no real Fred. Fred handles all of Ralph's financial data, bank accounts, business ledgers, etc. Fred talks like an accountant. He acts like an accountant. He even wears a suit like an accountant. He IS Ralph's financial data.

A group of executives are sitting around a table having a meeting. Only some of them are people, and some of them are Comms, running live projections of real humans, who are sitting miles away at their homes or wherever they are. There they see a projection of the meeting too, with Comms representing everyone who is not at their place. Thus, everyone sees the entire meeting, although in each location one or more of the people are really there and the rest are there (live) via comms.

Also there is Pete, he is the financial system. He isn't a person, he doesn't represent a person. He is, literally, the books. He is all the databases, financial records, bank records, etc, of the company. Operating independently, he talks like an accountant.

At one point, when the topic turns to the new Widgets, Fred, who has been pretty quiet up to that point, says, "Hey guys, I'm sorry but I can't really be with you today, but on the subject of the new Widgets, let me just say this ....", and Fred talks for a minute or two, in a prerecorded speech made the day before. Until that moment, the guy's didn't know Fred wasn't "really" there. They guy's thank Fred, comment on his comments, and continue the meeting. Fred was never there - the day before he said to his Comm, "Go to the meeting tomorrow for me, and if they start talking about the new Widgets, here is what I want to say ...."

Now the guys turn to Pete, and say "Pete, you heard Fred's proposal for new Widgets, what will that cost us? Can you project the profits into the next three quarters?". Pete talks, gives the data, and even holds up graphs and charts (just more holographic projections) for all to view.

A day later, Fred, the real Fred, asks his Comm to duplicate the entire meeting and Fred "attends" this meeting, in completely pre-recorded form! During this meeting, there are now two Freds! One is "in" the meeting, recorded as he was there, and the second is the live Fred, who is really there now. The live Fred can walk around in the meeting, but he cannot change it. His Comm body from yesterday is sitting there in the image even though his real body now walks around the pre-recorded hologram like a ghost attending the meeting! He shouts at them, but they act like they can't hear him! They can't!! Cool, eh?

A guy named Bob specializes in interviewing people to find out all about them. He asks them all kinds of basic questions, starting with "How old are you?", "Where were you born?", "Where did you go to school", "What subjects were you interested in?", etc. He gets them to tell him their entire life's story! He asks lots of personal questions. You don't have to answer any question you don't want to. If they are single, he asks them all kinds of questions about what they want in a mate too. If they are job-hunting, he can ask them all kinds of employment related questions.

Now of course, Bob doesn't actually "do" this anymore, his Comm does it, duplicating (recordings of) him doing it.

Now you can pay Bob (or his Comm, there is no longer any need to distinguish) to come and ask you these questions. And of course, you own Comm is "watching" and recording. So if anyone else wants to ask you one of these same questions at a later time, your Comm can just play the relevant parts of this recording for them.

Now, say you want to meet the entire line of chorus girls from Caesar's Palace in Las Vegas. You just ask your Comm to call them all, and a second later the entire chorus line is standing there in the room with you! (All of them are Comm of course!) You can have them perform a dance or two first. Then, you can walk up to one and talk to her. They have all had Bob ask them all his questions. Or maybe they've just been around enough to have been asked just about every question there is anyway, by somebody or other. Either way, their Comms "know" quite a lot about them. You can have a pretty detailed conversation with any of them!

After striking up a conversation with one who is particularly interesting, you can ask her to go live. Her Comm also is evaluating you, per profiles, and may reject you out of hand. But if not rejected, you may get to go "live" and find yourself talking with the "real" person (via a Comm projection in live mode)!

Thus a Comm is two things:

He or she communicates for you. A novelist's writings, a poet's poems, a story-teller's yarns, a dreamer's dreams, these ARE his Comm. The Comm replaces the book (and website etc). And when you die, your Comm can remain behind!

And the Comm is the computer of the future. You give it data, it analyses, computers, and tells you answers. It replaces concepts of the "human interface", the "operating system", the "keyboard and screen". After all, we talk about "natural" interfaces; what is the most natural thing there is for a human to talk to? Another "human", or in this case, "Comm."
