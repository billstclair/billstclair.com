Sensor, facsimile, perceptron

Definitions:

Sensor - any device connected to a WIZ which can input data about the physical universe. Examples: a switch, a button, a mouse, a pen, a scanner, a tv camera, a microphone, a position sensor, the rotational sensor on a motor axle, etc.

Facsimile - a memory which contains data from a sensor or sensors, as well as a record of the time & date is was recorded.

Thus, in the most usual sense, a facsimile is a picture of something taken by a camera. However, we will generalize it to mean any data taken from any sensor or sensors in the physical universe. It is a record of the physical universe, as sampled by whatever sensors a WIZ has, and recorded along with the time (date) of its occurrence.

Perceptron - a algorithm (program) which can analyze a facsimile and answer a specific question about it. Each perceptron answers a different specific question. The output of a perceptron (or the answer to the question) is generally either a yes/no bit or a number on a scale from 0 to 100. Examples:

  • a perceptron that looks at data from a microphone and looks for a one second beep sound at exactly middle C. That is all it does. It "recognizes" when a one second beep of middle C occurs.
  • a perceptron that looks at data from a mouse and outputs whether the button is pressed, and what x,y coordinate the mouse now represents.
  • a perceptron that looks at a keyboard and recognizes when a key is pressed and outputs which key.
  • a perceptron that looks at data from a microphone and recognizes the word "hello".
  • a perceptron that looks at data from a camera and recognizes if a human being is close by.
  • a perceptron that looks at data from a camera and recognizes if a male human being is close by.
  • a perceptron that looks at data from a camara and recognizes if a female human being under the age of 10 and with red hair is close by.
