The WIZ Power System

Regarding the battery running out. WIZes run forever! Seriously! There are two pieces to this: low power requirements in the first place, and new kinds of power sources in the second.

Low Power Requirements:

A WIZ processor can slow down to zero Hz, and wake on interrupt. Power usage is exactly linear to speed. At absolute zero Hz, it will use absolutely no power. It can sweep its cache RAM out to the Central Memory, and then doesn't even have to keep a D-RAM powered. Many WIZ applications can sleep most of the time. If I had a WIZ in my shoe. for instance, and it had a MEMS accelerometer to sense footsteps, it could wake just for a few nanoseconds at the peak of each footstep to record the data, then sleep until the next footstep. (Actually, it would be more interesting than that - it could change its speed in something like an adaptive sine wave, relating speed on a moment-by-moment basis to what its doing). A WIZ-light switch would only execute when the switch handle was jarred. (It might run at 100 Hz while waiting, just so it could keep monitoring everything). All WIZs slow down the CPU while waiting for an external event to wake it. In fact, whenever the processor goes into a "wait state" for I/O, it can cycle down speed and wake on interrupt from the device in question. Thus, power used will be often minuscule. (Its in a different chapter - "The WIZ-object", but most WIZes will be inside shoes, switches, staplers, whatever. Not "windows" machines running screens with multiple processes all going like crazy). Obviously, on a cpu bound application, this is not true, but that kind of application will be plugged in!

There will be an additional register in the standard FB1 which specifies the speed, all ones being max, and all zeroes being zero. At exact zero, only an interrupt can wake it up. Note that the nanosecond clock register also in the FB1 can also wake it on countdown, and is not shut off when the processor speed goes to zero -- although it too CAN be shut down too, if the target count register is zero also.

New Power Sources to be investigated:

Secondly, I will be investigating a number of new kinds of power supplies. Since all isolated WIZes will have a radio link, they can pick up power over that link by using the radio signal itself as power (as Tesla always planned). It may be possible to keep powered by this alone. It may be possible to get enough energy by induction from the 60 Hz field that is all around us. It may be possible to "recharge" it occasionally with a higher than normal burst of radio power (at night or when no humans are around!).

Beyond that, new power sources have yet to be discovered. I believe cold fusion is a real phenomenon, and will be perfected. It could be applied on a MEMS nano-scale to make a cold fusion reactor a few nanometers big.
 

I have also read the very theoretical idea of creating a "bowling alley" sort of MEMS structure a few nanometers wide, and accelerating single particles down the alley to a target, thus creating a very tiny but super-high energy particle accelerator, which would create hot (thermo-nuclear) fusion, but in non-dangerous quantities of a single atom at a time. Wouldn't that be far out!

And room-temperature superconducting nanotubes are right around the corner already.

And "transistors" of the future, or whatever may come, may use significantly less power than anything we can envision now.

The WIZ is a device for the future, and I am certain that the future holds plenty of possibilities like the above. So much so that the WIZ is designed from the start to assume that one or more of these technologies WILL be available.

In the short run, I think the radio link will do the trick for truly remote WIZes that have no other connections. Note that all WIZes have some sort of comm line, and if not radio, then there IS some sort of wire, and then power is easy to supply over that wire. Thus, all the above discussion is not about desktop computers running Windows. They can have plenty of power. It is for the truly remote or portable WIZ this discussion is for, the WIZ in the shoe, switch, turn signal, etc. The WIZ in a light bulb obviously has plenty of power available to it as the light itself will need plenty of power. Of course, as the above technologies really do become real, maybe even that will go away. Who knows how much power I can get out of a MEMS thermo-nuclear reactor using molecules right out of the air for power?
