On my last day of my visit to the Ukraine, Thursday, October 26, 2000, I bought some Russian nested dolls in an outdoor market in Lviv near the Opera House. I have heard these called Mamushka, Babushka, and Matrushka, but it appears from a quick Google search that Matrushka is correct, so that's what I'll use (though I named the jpegs babushka). I gave these dolls to my daughter Victoria. She loves them. So with no further ado, here they are. Click on any picture for a larger version.
