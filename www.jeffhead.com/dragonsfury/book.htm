About the Book

This book was originally written a five part series in 1999 and 2000. The first in that series was self-published in 2001. After publishing the rest, I created a new edition and combined all five novels into one hardback novel, which is what is currently selling.

A lot of time has passed. Sadly, many of the events spoken of in these novels have come about in terms of the growth of Islamic Terror, the growth in technological capabilities of potential adversary nations of the U.S., the continued erosin of the US southern border, continued erision of constitutional rights for the sake of security, and many of the advancement the US military has made in the intervening years. So the novel has conintued to be applicable to today's events.

It was also written unapologetically from a US Consitutional perspective and some of the reviews on Amazon and elsewhere reflect a few readers' dissasitfaction with that aspect of the novel. I make no aoplogies. All I can say is that everyone has a right to their own beliefs. I poured out some of mine into this novel, along with what is rather widely acclaimed as a very exciting, technological, global war story.

To this day I hope readers will enjoy it.

Here's what was written ten years ago as a teaser for the book:

During a false peace…war breaks out! After great success in the War on Terror, the politicians and the analysts thought that another global conflict was impossible. They were wrong!

Dragon's Fury - World War against America and the West projects a future arising out of current events where:

  • Implacable enemies of America develop surprising and very lethal high-technology military capabilities of their own.
  • America's unsecured southern border allows tens of thousands of terrorists to infiltrate into the nation.
  • The New Economy of off-shore manufacturing and outsourcing severely curtails domestic production and bolsters future enemies.
  • Citizens' constitutional rights are eroded in a vain effort to ensure domestic security.
  • Three generations of Americans never challenged for survival, must take up arms and fight for their very existence.
Come on this journey. Can America find the strength and the faith to prevail in a world-wide conflagration? If you have ever pondered issues like these, and if you enjoy an exciting thriller, you owe it to yourself to read Dragon's Fury - World War against America and the West...and if you do ponder such issues, then just imagine:
What if an entire future Presidential election cycle in the United States went unexpectedly and entirely awry?

What if all of the radical Islamic nations united and then allied themselves with Red China?

What if the North Koreans were emboldened to make a massive invasion of South Korea?

Dragon's Fury - World War against America and the West, is that story…

It is a story of competing cultures and ideologies, of strategies and technology, where the Red Chinese, the radical Islamic nations and other belligerents develop surprising technological advances that they mix with assymetrical warfare techniques and employ against the West. It is about suppositions, arrogance, and the fact that neither side is going to do what the other side expects, no matter how good the plan.

It is a story of faith and the ultimate triumph of good over evil in the face of horrific obstacles and setbacks. From leaders in high offices of power, to the soldiers and sailors of the involved nations, to housewives and their children, down to kids on the street, it is also the story of people - common and not so common - caught up in the titanic struggle.

It sees the United States of America savaged by manipulating, committed, and ruthless foreign leaders, and that savaging ultimately produces the will and fortitude of another "greatest generation".

The novel addresses key politcal, societal, and military issues including the inherant dangers of off-shore outsourcing, the dangers of uncontrolled borders, the impact of unbridled military downsizing, the pitfalls of over reliance on high technology, methods of applying "Homeland Security" so as to avoid abuse, and how firm societal moral foundations make a pivotal difference in a nation's ability to maintain a prolonged war effort.

May the reading of this novel give folks pause as they contemplate the potential for horror in today's current events, may they be heartened by the strength and vitality of a good, moral people, and may they be motivated to remain strong and committed to liberty...a liberty based on the unalienable rights of the individual, on the free will of those same individuals, on morality, on faith in the Hand of Providence, and on individual accountability...at all levels.
