As a security and IT security professional, here are a few things that all Americans can do to try and protect themselves, and to fight back.

Trying to protect your personal communications:

  1. Have a personal computer totally unattached to the "net" or any other network.
  2. Have an email/on line presence that is annonymous and not associated with your own name, address, or any other personally identifiable information, and create it at a public internet cafe you never go back to.
  3. Any normal interaction that you do not mind having recorded, carry on with your cell phone, land line, home/business internet as normal.
  4. Any political or other private information should be authored and written on your non-connected computer, and then taken to a public internet location for transmittal. Never use the same location in succession, and make them as far away from where you actually live as possible, with no "pattern" that can zero in your place of residence. This will require some thought and planning.
  5. Any and all political/private communications should be conducted through your annonymous online presence, and, as above, do it at public internet cafe...never posittng from the same place in succession.
  6. Any and all political/private phone communications, use a throw away cell phone and have it tied to a psuedo identity that is not your own. Discard them after use.
  7. Do not establish any patterns with your annonymous use. Those recording/monitoring programs will know where you annonymous identity posts/emails/calls from and to and WILL track it.

Be prepared to go permanently offline in this manner if necessary. That's a sacrifice...but, in reality, this is a war.
