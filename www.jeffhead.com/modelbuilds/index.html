The Hobby Shop - My hobby shop has been created from a section of our master bedroom using ceiling height shelving as a wall to seperate the shop. These shelves hold the models which have already been built and will ultimately be replaced by wider shelves capable of holding Glassed-in Display Cases for each Model. These cases will either be custom built to all be the same, or purchased from a supplier that has the various sizes needed in the same style. The picture above shows the basics of the shop itself where a model building table is located, with shelving above, and a computer desk and computer for conducting research on the net to the right of the building/construction area.


The overall Shop, the containers holding the paints,glues, and putty, and the containers holding the tools.


Current paint brush holder (new brushes are stored in the desk drawer), shelves holding Rubber bands, Decals, Photo Etch Sets, and spare parts.

The Stash - Models purchased since the early 1990s, and many over the last two years for completion of my 1/350 Scale World-wide Carrier Strike Group (CSG) Project. These are to be built over the next several years and they line the two upper shelves across the whole length of the building/construction table, and then underneath the table as well. These include 1/350 scale ships and 1/72 scale aircraft.


The entire upper two shelves of the stash.


The British and Japanes CSGs. For the UK it is the Hobby Boss HMS Astute SSN, the Airfix HMS Trafalgar SSN, Two Trumpter Duke Class FFGs, Two Airfix Daring Class DDGs, and the Airfix HMS Illustrious. For the Japanese, it's the Pit Road, JMSDF Hakurya SSK, the Trumpeter JMSDF Takanami DDG, two Trumpeter Atago DDGs, and the Fujimi JMSDF Hyuga DDH.


The French CSG. For the French all of the models are Heller, 1/400 scale. The Duquesne DDG, the Duguay Trouin DDG, two La Faytette FFGs and the Charles de Gaulle CVN.


The Russian CGS and the start of the Chinese Amphibious Group (ARG). Also shown for the Chinese is the Bronco Chinese Yuan SSK, the Trumperter Chinese Shenyang DDG and the Trumpeter Ningbo DDG.
