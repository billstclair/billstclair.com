 LINKS 		The Coalition of the Willing
 
 The United States and President Bush assembled a Coalition to disarm Iraq of its weapons of mass destruction, and enforce 17 UNSC resolutions. The Coalition also liberated the Iraqi people from one of the worst tyrants and most brutal regimes on earth.
 
 Contributions from Coalition member nations ranged from: direct military participation, logistical and intelligence support, specialized chemical/biological response teams, over-flight rights, humanitarian and reconstruction aid, to political support.
