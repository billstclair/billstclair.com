This site's mission is to document and analyse the AEGIS and AEGIS-like multi-role, but principally anti-air escort vessels of the world. AEGIS-like is defined here as a major combatant used as an escort vessel that incorporates the following into its design: Phased Array Radar System (PARS), Vertical Launch System (VLS) for it's primary missile battery, and a digitially controlled, fully integrated battle management system capable of controlling all phases of combat, potentially digitally linked to other vessels for cooperative engagements.
