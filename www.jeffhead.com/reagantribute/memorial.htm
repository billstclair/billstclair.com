 LINKS 		Memorial   - On June 5th, 2004, Ronald Reagan passed from this earth. He died as a result of complications due to Alzheimers disease, while Nancy was at his side. Despite having not opened his eyes for many days, one of his final acts was to open his eyes and look clearly, intelligently, and with love into Nancy's eyes, and then pass away. Nancy indicated that this was one of the greatest last gifts he or the Lord could have given her.

The following pictures and remarks, marked the week-long mourning period for this great man. Hundreds of thousands made their way to California and Washington DC to pay their respects. The mourning period culminated in his state funeral in Washgton DC on Friday, June 101th, and his sunset burial in California that same evening.

LYING IN REPOSE IN CALIFORNIA. JUNE 7-9, 2004:


Tens of thousands, then hundreds of thousands waited in line to pay their respects.


After the viewing, as Nancy was comforted, the President was moved from his library.


As tens of thousands more stood by, Nancy and the President were flown to Washington DC.

LYING IN STATE IN WASHINTON DC, JUNE 9-10, 2004:



In Washington DC, the President traveled through lined streets on a caisson to the rotunda.



Again, many tens of thousands waited in line to pay their respects to President Reagan.



Gorbachev, Lech Walesa, Margaret Thatcher, former Pres Bush, Pres Bush and Nancy at the casket

ADDRESSES WHILE THE PRESIDENT WAS IN STATE
Vice President Cheney's Remarks - Representative Hastert's Remarks

FUNERAL IN WASHINGTON DC, JUNE 11, 2004:


President Reagan is brought into the National Cathedral and Pres. Bush seats Nancy.


Five presidents attend the wonderful and moving cerimony of faith, prayer, song and euology.


Thatcher, Mulroney, former Pres. Bush, Pres. Bush & Min. Danforth speak at the service.

ADDRESSES AT THE PRESIDENT'S FUNERAL
Former PM Thatcher - Former PM Mulroney - Former Pres. Bush
Pres. Bush Eulogy - Former Sen. Danforth Homily

BURIAL IN CALIFORNIA, SUNSET ON JUNE 11, 2004:




Tens, perhaps hundreds of thousands lined the roads as the President was driven back to his library.



A beautiful service of prayer, song and his children's remarks was held overlooking the Pacific Ocean.

ADDRESSES AT THE PRESIDENT'S BURIAL
Michael Reagan - Patti Davis (Reagan) - Ron Reagan




One last prayer & paying of respect, one last moment for the family & then the flag is passed to Nancy.


As the sun sets over the Pacific Ocean, Ronald Wilson Reagan finds his rest looking to the golden west.
