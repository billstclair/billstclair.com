 LINKS 		Tribute to Ronald Reagan (Last Update: 01/24/05)  - This site was created by Jeff Head to commemorate the life and legacy of a great American, the 40th President of the United States, Ronald Wilson Reagan (1911-2004). May the pictures, his speeches, and the tributes and eulogies be an inspiration to all Americans, particularly the rising generation. May all be motivated to study his gentle, faithful, Christian attributes, and his commitment to liberty and the moral principles that define it.



May we each understand, as he did, that all mankind originates from God, that they have a mission and purpose in life, and that the quest for personal excellence, virtue, goodness, liberty and justice are worthy of sacrifice and commitment. This is what Ronald Reagan did, and in so doing, he showed what tremendous impact a single individual can have, and liberated tens of millions souls from bondage and tyranny.
