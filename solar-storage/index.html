Solar Pole Barn

Bill St. Clair, Saturday, 14 July, 2012

My neighbor, Wali, has been working for a long time on plans for a solar pole barn, to supply part of the power needs of the Abode of the Message, and for storage. Frank, of Jones Boys Construction, who I still think of as "Weasel", though he's been discouraging that nickname of late, has been working for the last week preparing the ground for it. He finished yesterday, and the building materials were delivered. Below are some photos. Click on any one for a higher-resolution version (most 1200x900 and 350K to 550K in size).

Construction is scheduled to begin on Monday. I'll add more photos as it progresses.
