It's Here, at Last
A Review of Innocents Betrayed
by
Sunni Maravillosa

The "it" I refer to is Innocents Betrayed, the long-awaited JPFO documentary presenting the inevitable outcome of gun control. I can't in all honesty say the film was pleasant to watch -- despite an ongoing conversation with screenwriter Claire Wolfe about it during its production I was still unprepared for its impact -- but I can assure you that any individual with integrity who sees it will have to grant that firearms do have a legitimate place in private individuals' hands.
