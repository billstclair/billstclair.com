Sunni Maravillosa has focused her writing on research and other nonfiction-oriented areas. A former professor, her sharp eye is well-practiced at catching inconsistencies and fixing grammar.

Carl Bussjaeger's writing has been concentrated - if you can call it that - on political and social commentary, science fiction, and assorted types of tutorials. Before regaining his senses, he tried to serve at both the state and federal levels of government; but now stays strictly in the private, free market, sector.

Samuel Paine has probably forgotten more about computers than I'll ever know. Even so, he does all the "heavy lifting" on the Web site, writing and testing the cgi scripts, and catching the little goofs that occasionally slip past me. His contributions to DF! are largely behind the scenes, but vital and greatly appreciated.
