A window into Communism's heart

cold as ice on pluto and hard as ironstone
as loving as stalin with the old Bolsheviks
warmth of the gulags in siberia
as sweet as the red guard
compassionate as the chimer rouge in killing fields
as warm as mao as he murdered 100 million
over flowing with the love beria gave girls he raped

Peter Wilhelm
