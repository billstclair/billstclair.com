Doing Freedom! Funding Poll
Maybe "information wants to be free," but generating and publishing it cost money. Doing Freedom! is seeking ways to pay its way. Some of the options being considered are listed below. Please check the option you would prefer.

Monthly subscription issues only. The existing archive of past issues would remain public. Subscriptions would probably be in the $15-$25/year range.

Monthly subscription issues, with smaller quarterly public issues. Subscriptions would include additional services not given to non-subscribers. Subscriptions would probably be in the $10-$20/year range.

Continue on present free content course, with monthly calls for donations. I would donate.

Continue on present free content course, with monthly calls for donations. I would NOT donate.

Don't bother readers with calls for donations. Continue free until DF! folds.
