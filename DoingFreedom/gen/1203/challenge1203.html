The Monthly Challenge
Improving your shooting skills
by
Columba

It's been fun playing with pistols, but the recent Supreme Court denial of cert for Silveira vs Lockyer just gives me an itch to have my battle rifle back in my hands. I have an easier challenge for this month, and it's related to this month's article of mine. Without further ado, here it is:

Go to a shooting range where you can post targets at multiple different ranges. Set up silhouettes at 25 yards, 50 yards, 100 yards, and as close to 300 yards as you can get. Now, can you put 2 or 3 shots in each one in the space of a 90 seconds? Yes, that includes the time to move from range to range. Use any position you like - the key is to know your sights.
