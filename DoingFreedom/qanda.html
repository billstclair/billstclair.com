Why isn't there a new issue up yet?
We are currently publishing DF! on a bimonthly schedule--every other month we'll post a new issue. We don't have a specific date of the month on which we publish, and it will vary from issue to issue. If a new issue is scheduled to come out this month, please be patient and check back often--unless there's been a problem, there'll be a new issue up soon! [to question list]

Why don't you update your Web site more often?
Well, we could publish articles as soon as we accept them, and have fresh content much more frequently. But we've chosen not to, because we like the 'zine format. We know we can't compete with World Net Daily and Sierra Times, and we're not trying to. We think that the 'zine structure, with a known publishing time frame and lots of new material, works better for what we're doing--providing information on how to be freer in every aspect of your life. We do update the site when there's breaking news, or when a new feature is added, but we don't make a habit of it. Having this structure makes it easier for you to find new articles when they're published, and gives you lots of good information to read for the next several days. [to question list]

What's your publication schedule?
We're currently publishing Doing Freedom! on a bimonthly schedule--every other month, there's a new issue of DF! published. We would like to see DF! become monthly, but frankly, that will require more contributors, and more income, from both subscribers and advertisers, to happen. [to question list]

Why do you ask people to subscribe?
It may seem strange that we offer our terrific content for free, and ask people to subscribe... but it makes sense to us. First, we know there's a lot of hype on the Web--a lot of sites that promise all kinds of things, but fail to deliver. We aren't one of them, and we never will be. We want that to be obvious to anyone who visits our site. Making our articles publicly available accomplishes that. Also, we know this is information that many people really want, and while we like to earn money for what we're doing, it's more important to us that people can create the freedom they want.
We offer a subscriber version--with extras that aren't available to those who browse the free site--because we pay our contributors for their work. We pay for our articles because we think that's the best way to ensure high quality, and also because doing so helps enable others to create income streams that are free from the grabbing hand of the state. We can't afford to do this on our own--we aren't rich--so we ask that those who find value in our service pay for it, just like you subscribe to a local newspaper or Reason magazine. By subscribing to DF!, you are helping to create more freedom, and are doing freedom yourself! [to question list]

What do subscribers get for their money?
We currently offer several perks for subscribers, including a downloadable .zip file of each issue for convenient offline reading, a Reading Room with hard-to-find articles on relevant topics and other special features, a bulletin board for communicating with other pro-freedom individuals, free classified ads, and, of course, articles that are free of advertising. Other benefits are being planned, and will be forthcoming. [to question list]

Who writes for you?
Anyone who has something valuable to contribute! We like--and value greatly--articles from Claire Wolfe, Vin Suprynowicz, and Patty Neill, among other notables, but will give consideration to any relevant, well-written article from anyone. If you're interested in sending us an article, please see our writer's guidelines. [to question list]

What are your publication requirements?
We have only two requirements: that the article be well-written and researched (if relevant), and that it be consistent with our mission of doing freedom. We won't accept rants or highly theoretical articles that don't have action-oriented content. We do accept fiction and other forms of artwork that has pro-freedom themes (e.g., cartoons, graphics of paintings or sculptures, etc.). If you're thinking of sending us something, please see our writer's guidelines and our information on how to send us your material for consideration. [to question list]

Why are you publishing this stuff?
We believe that freedom is the natural state of human beings--freedom is necessary for people to be their best and healthiest. Unfortunately, much of every culture we know of is inimical to freedom. Almost everywhere one encounters threats to one's freedom, be it in friendships, family relationships, or the state's attempts to regulate, monitor, and control. We write about, and publish material on freedom in order to reach as many people as possible, to teach them the value of freedom, and how to create more of it in their lives. The more people who live in freedom, the better for us all. [to question list]

Aren't you afraid the govgoons will come after you?
"Afraid" isn't exactly the right word. We know that they've visited this site--lots of 'em. What they're likely to do on the basis of what we publish here is anyone's guess. But we won't let the possibility of threats, smear campaigns, or Wacoing keep us from doing what we know is right. We are peacefully disseminating information on how to be free--much of which the thought police would rather you not know, or not think about. That's because those who try to rule others need and/or enjoy the power they get from doing so, and we are a very real threat to that power.
The nation-state as we know it is dying. We think that it's in its death throes--hence their fear. But we view what we're doing as being in the proud tradition of Benjamin Tucker and others who resist the rule of others by some. It's likely they'll come after us, but so be it. We won't back down, we won't be cowed... and we won't be ruled by fear. [back to question list]
