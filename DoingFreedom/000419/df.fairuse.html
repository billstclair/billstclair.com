Fair Use Policy

If you like what you see here, that's great! And if you want to share it with others, that's even better; we want our material to be widely circulated. As long as you follow some basic, commonsense guidelines for doing so, you're welcome to share DF! material with anyone you think will value it.

You may copy entire articles, or quote from articles for distribution as long as you:

  • copy or quote verbatim;
  • clearly credit the author of the piece, and Doing Freedom! magazine as the sources;
  • if the author has provided an e-mail address, include it for contact--if not, include Sunni@free-market.net (Sunni Maravillosa) as a contact regarding the work; and
  • provide the URL for DF's home page (http://www.doingfreedom.com/).

If you post an article or piece of artwork from DF! on your site, a link to DF! is highly encouraged.
