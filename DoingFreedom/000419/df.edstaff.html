Don Lobo Tiggre (Tiggre@free-market.net) has been writing pro-freedom fiction and nonfiction for many years, in various capacities and under different names. He's worn the editor hat before, too, and hasn't had a writer threaten him with a weapon yet. Sunni Maravillosa (Sunni@free-market.net) has focused her writing on research and other nonfiction-oriented areas. A former professor, her sharp eye is well-practiced at catching inconsistencies and fixing grammar. Sunni's also the one who webbifies all the material for use on DF!, so if you spot problems of that nature, please contact her about them.

Sunni and Don Lobo prefer that all correspondence be encrypted using PGP; accordingly, their PGP keys are below for your use. An acceptable alternative that provides some security is to use Hushmail for contacting them (it's free, web-based, and quite easy to use). DLT's hushmail account is DonLobo@hushmail.com, and Sunni's is Sunni-Snake@hushmail.com.
