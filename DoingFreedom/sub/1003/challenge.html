The Monthly Challenge
Improving your shooting skills
by
Columba

How did you do on the last month's challenge, the Army Qualification drill? Did you make Expert? If so, awesome! If not, keep on practicing and try again every so often.

After reading Matthew Bracken's book, Enemies Foreign and Domestic, I thought we might take a cue from it for this month's shooting challenge, and see how well we know our pistols. So grab a silhouette and put it up at the 100-yard line. Now pull out your carry pistol, and see how many hits you can make (resting the barrel on a makeshift support is fine, but using a bench rest is an awfully wimpy way out...). Real hotshots can try it standing or sitting without a rest. And hey, if you've got the equipment, go ahead and try to replicate Ranya Bardiwell's shot. You'll have to get the book to find out just what her shot was, of course... ;)
