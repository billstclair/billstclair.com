Social Security Number Not Required
by
DaOptimist

I recently received my EMT license in one of the largest counties in America. This is not necessarily a huge accomplishment, however I managed to receive this license without having to use a social security number. I am going to tell you how I acquired my license without submitting the required number and how you can do the same whenever you are told, "I'm sorry, we can't help you. A social security number is required.

My first step was to call the licensing office and explain that I fully intended to become a licensed EMT, but since I do not use a social security number, I needed to know what number to use in lieu of said number. I made sure to talk to the person that was in authority to issue the license. I was told, in no uncertain terms, that it would be impossible for them to issue a license without the required social security number. I was also advised to try another county because the county in which I live in "has never and will never issue a license without" the required number.

REMEMBER: Nothing is impossible! Whenever you are told that ANYTHING is required, ALWAYS question that authority.

My next step was to respond with the following letter that is loosely structured from one found on the WEPIN website. Feel free to copy any or all portions of this letter. I have added some explanatory notes in parentheses.

Dear <REDACTED> -

(Start off with a being polite. This will throw them off guard and also help to bring them around to your point of view. As Stephen Covey says, "Seek first to understand.")

First I would like to apologize profusely for having caught you in a rushed moment this morning. I realize that my situation is a little more involved than most and can not be resolved in a brief conversation on the telephone.

(State your understanding of their reason for refusal and then state your reasons for your belief of not using a social security number.)

I understand your position that "certification in <BIG COUNTY> is not done without a Social Security number". However, it is a violation of my principles and beliefs to participate in a universal numbering system. I know that the law allows for me to not be discriminated upon for many reasons and one of those is for my beliefs.

(A little more sugar always helps to grease the wheels. Since they are obviously unfamiliar with this situation, ALWAYS advise them to consult with their legal counsel if they are uncertain with how to meet YOUR requirement of NOT submitting a social security number. Offer the solutions that I included in my letter.)

I am not sure how rare my situation is but from our conversation this morning, it appears that you have not dealt with this situation in the past. I apologize if I caught you off guard.

I am sorry for adding to your probably overwhelming workload and I hope that we can resolve this expediently. Would you please research the matter or consult with your legal counsel in regards to what options that I must take in order to obtain my EMT-B certification in . Perhaps an internal number can be issued to me for purposes of certification or the "requirement" can be waived in this case.

(Demand that they back up their requirement with the EXACT statute that backs up their claim of a requirement.)

If you continue to insist that a person MUST obtain and use a Social Security number in order to be certified within , please send me copies of any relevant statutes that directly pertain to the fact that a person is REQUIRED to obtain and use a Social Security number for these purposes.

(Set a time limit.)

I will expect your response to my request to receive information on how I may obtain my EMT-B certification without use of a Social Security number or copies of relevant statutes that show that a person is REQUIRED to obtain and use a Social Security number strictly for certification purposes within 30 days of your receipt of this e-mail. If you need additional time, please notify me by e-mail or in writing to the address below.

(Change the presumption.)

If I do not hear from you within that time, your lack of response will establish the presumption that you or your Department do not have the documentation or the authority to support your claim of any requirement upon me.

(More politeness. This is my secret ingredient. You must remain firm and stand on your principles, but ALWAYS buffer the firmness with kindness and politeness.)

I want to thank you again for taking the time to accept my call this morning and for trying to help me to find a resolution as soon as possible,

Sincerely,

     DaOptimist

The result of this letter was that I obtained my license without having to obtain a socialist number. Not only that, but according to the licensing department's original claim, I achieved the "impossible." In addition, I received a very nice congratulatory e-mail from the head of the licensing agency for passing my licensing exam with high marks and I was thanked for my "kind words and understanding."

One more tip that I did not use but that I highly recommend, send this letter via registered letter instead of e-mail.

Good luck!
