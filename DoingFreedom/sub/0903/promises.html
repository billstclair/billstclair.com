Promises, Promises
by
Sunni Maravillosa

For those of who (all four ;-) who've been waiting for my much-mentioned book The Forgotten Freedom to appear here, I offer my apologies. I had planned to have the book completed by Labor Day ... and, in usual freedom-activist fashion, promptly overcommitted myself to projects. I'll also confess that some summer days it was tough for me to be optimistic about freedom's chances.

So, I have taken a page from my own book (well, okay, not a literal page, because I haven't gotten that far yet) and tried to make positive changes in my life. And I'm feeling better about that. I have also learned how to say "no" to more people than just my children, and unless my day job implodes and I need to don my professor's garb or flip burgers to support them, I ought to have The Forgotten Freedom completed by Newton's birthday (which also happens to be Christmas, for those of you of the nonscientific/trivia-collecting persuasions). That means it should be available in the first DF! of '04. But I'm not making any promises.

Thanks for your patience. I hope you find it well rewarded.

-- Sunni
