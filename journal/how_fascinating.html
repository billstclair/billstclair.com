How Fascinating

Submitted by Bill St. Clair on Mon, 09 Mar 2015 14:41:24 GMT  <== Poetry ==> 


HOW FASCINATING

How

Fascinating the idea of death

Can be.

Too bad, though,

Because

It just isn't

True.


Daniel Ladinsky, The Gift
