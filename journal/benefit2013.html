Victoria's Benefit Concert Bash

Submitted by Bill St. Clair on Wed, 09 Jan 2013 12:10:42 GMT  <== Glory ==> 

My daughter has been suffering with Lyme Disease and tick-borne parasites for almost four years now. Her condition has improved, but she is still unable to be physically or mentally active for more than a few hours a day. Paying for her medical care, even with lots of help from insurance, has drained my savings. So my son, Christopher, has organized a benefit concert and feast. It's this coming Saturday, January 12, 2013. Details at benefit2013.nfshost.com, and on its Facebook event page.

We are soliciting donations, at the event if you can make it, or via Chipin (widget below requires Flash) or a check in the mail if not (see other pages for who to pay and where to mail).
