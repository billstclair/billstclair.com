David Grover at the Bandstand

Submitted by Bill St. Clair on Sat, 25 Aug 2012 08:37:55 GMT  <== Glory ==> 

Two weeks back, on a Friday night, I went to a Linda Worster gig at the Great Barrington bandstand. David Grover showed up, played some tunes with Linda, and talked for over half an hour afterwards. I learned that for over 20 years, he's been doing a show every summer Saturday morning at that bandstand. So I showed up the next morning, on my new scooter, and went home with an Official David Grover Beanie Cap (pictured below with my scooter in the background). I went again last Saturday, and took the photos below. I plan to go today, for the last show of the summer. Hey, I'm just a big kid.
