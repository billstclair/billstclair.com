Newt and Turtle

Submitted by Bill St. Clair on Thu, 12 Jul 2012 12:24:36 GMT  <== Glory ==> 

Below are a couple of photos I took on May 31, on the dirt road where I walk the dogs, an orange newt and a (snapping?) turtle. Both cooperated well with the photgraphy process, remaining motionless the whole time. The newt was perhaps six inches long, from tip to tail. The turtle was over a foot across. (S)he was crossing the road to the pond, when I rode by on my scooter.

"Newt and Turtle" reminds me of Boris Badenoff in the Bullwinkle & Rocky show saying "Moose and Squirrel" with a Russian accent.
