How Do I Listen?

Submitted by Bill St. Clair on Thu, 28 Jun 2012 13:01:08 GMT  <== Poetry ==> 

How

Do I

Listen to others?

As if everyone were my Master

Speaking to me

His

Cherished

Last

Words.

[from The Gift, versions of Hafiz by Daniel Ladinsky]
