Methamphetamine Anti-Proliferation Act of 1999

This page is a repository of communications concering H.R.2987, the so-called Methamphetamine Anti-Proliferation Act of 1999. I say "so-called" because this bill contains blatantly unconstitutional provisions which make it illegal to demonstrate or publish a recipe for any controlled substance in any form. Highly illegal. A felony punishable by 10 years in prison. The senate version of this bill requires ISPs to shut down a web site if a law enforcement officer alleges that it contains a link to a drug paraphernalia site. Within 48 hours. No court, no warrant, nothing.

I sent a letter via one of the anti-prohibition sites to my congress critter, John E. Sweeney. I didn't get a copy of the email sent to him, likely because he doesn't have email, so the letter was likely faxed. I DO have a copy of the letters to senators Schumer and Moynihan, and they should contain identical text (I say "should" only because I haven't seen the letter to Mr. Sweeney. I have no reason to believe that his was different). The letter to Senator Moynihan is here. Its first two paragraphs are compliments of the anti-prohibition site. The last paragraph is mine.

Mr Sweeney wrote me back, less than a week after I sent my letter. He is one of the sponsors of this horrific legislation, and he said that he supports the most horific parts of it. Too bad.
