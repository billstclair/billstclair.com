The Webley Mark VI .455 .45 ACP in all its glory.

You may be able to tell from this photo how the clips work. I don't normally recommend setting a weapon down on naked concrete this way, but I was very careful and it seems to have worked out.

Too bad I had to give Roger back his gun. Anybody got a Mark VI they want to get rid of, preferably in the original .455? It's a good cartridge and very easy to reload. I use a 265 grain lead semiwadcutter, Lyman mould number 454424 and damned little powder.
