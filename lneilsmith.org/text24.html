Risks & Rewards (3)

  • “Decent Profit Scenario

  • Invest $5000

    • Book sells for $70,000 and film rights sell for $10,000: Total is $80,000
    • You get your principal back of $5000
    • Neil gets $15,000 profit (half the marginal profit after investors are paid back their principal)
    • You get 1/10th of the remaining profit, or $1500
    • Your return on investment is therefore 30%
