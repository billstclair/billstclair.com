Risks & Rewards

  • Risks

    • It is possible the book will not sell at all

  • Addressing Risk

    • Probability is low, due to Neil’s track record and the fact that publishers will know what they are buying - and “Ares” builds on both Ceres” and “Pallas”
    • The Ares Alliance, by its mere existence, will prove to publishers that this book has “legs” and has a ready market

  • Reward

    • Book will sell for higher-than-subsidized prices, film project will generate additional revenue
