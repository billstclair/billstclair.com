Financial and Control Plan

  • Funding goes to L. Neil Smith for living expenses as he writes the book

  • Every 3 months, investors call a Quarterly Status Meeting (conference call) to track progress

  • After four years from July 4, 2003 (or when the Plan is deemed “subscribed), Neil is obligated to pay back the investors

  • Marketing of the book starts before the book is completed, and includes mention of The Ceres Project syndicate and The Ares Alliance!
