"Ares Alliance" Reaches $16,000 In Committed Funds

Target Goal of $50,000 On the Horizon

For Immediate Release

Ft. Collins, CO and Austin, TX (NetNewsDaily)—The Ares Alliance, an investment club to help fund the creation of the book and film treatment "Ares", has announced that it has reached $16,000 in committed funds out of its target goal of $50,000.

"Your continuing support and praise for my efforts is the most heartening thing I could possibly imagine", said leading award- winning author and long-time libertarian sage L. Neil Smith. "And by the way, I'm not an herb, so don't call me 'sage', OK?", Smith quipped.

"The Ares Alliance is a chance for extraordinary people to participate in the creation of a major new work of importance, and to participate in profits, too", explained Ares Alliance Chained-to-A- Chair Alan R. Weiss. "Seriously, if you were too late to participate in "Ceres", if you were thinking about participating in "Ares", now is the time to join up. At the rate we're going, "Ares" may be fully funded quicker than we think", noted Weiss. "You don't have to fund the entire amount, either - you can invest over time."
