Project Ceres


The Ceres Project is Completely Funded

January 27, 2003

Dear Friends,

It is with great pleasure that I announce that The Ceres Project has been completely funded to $50,000, enabling L. Neil Smith to write the upcoming book Ceres as well as the film treatment. Neil has asked me to make this announcement, and to also inform everyone that the fund is now closed.

Our first virtual meeting for the investors will take place on Wednesday. Information has been sent out to those who have invested. If you have any doubts, please contact me directly.

L. Neil Smith and I will thank you in the most sincere way we possibly can - by him producing a work we can all be proud of, a work that we hope will break through previous barriers and lead to much broader dissemination of our ideas, and a work that will be commercially successful as well.

Neil has another project in mind for those of you who did not receive an official "Welcome to Ceres Project" letter from myself, so despair not! Any good talent needs to work on more than one thing at a time to keep it all fresh, and Neil is no exception.

Thank you, one and all.

Alan R. Weiss                  L. Neil Smith
                  The Ceres Project
