Risks & Rewards (4)

  • “Expected Profit Scenario

  • Invest $5000

    • Book sells for $70,000 and film rights sell for $20,000, audio and ebook rights are $10,000: Total is $100,000
    • You get your principal back of $5000
    • Neil gets $25,000 profit (half the marginal profit after investors are paid back their principal)
    • You get 1/10th of the remaining profit, or $2500
    • Your return on investment is therefore 50%
