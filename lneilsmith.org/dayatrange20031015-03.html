Here's my Heckler und Koch P9S .45, a weapon for which the manufacturer seems to have nothing but contempt. It's a little hard to control in recoil, owing to its light front end, but it's very strong (it has the typical H&K roller locking system) and exceedingly pleasant to carry. It's my lightest .45 auto, and I keep it that way by carrying Glaser Safety Slugs in it. It's the only .45 I have that doesn't care for either shape of HydraShok.

This is also the one I want a couple more spare magazines for, although they don't seem to exist.

But I'm sending this picture mainly because of the holster, which I'll describe in the next page.
