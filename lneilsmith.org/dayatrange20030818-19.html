The obligatory "Proud Owner" shot.

The EAA Witness .45/10mm is relatively heavy and shoots straight and easy. Of all my various CZ75 clones, it has by far the best trigger (although my Springfield Armory P9 .40 comes close).

Eleven rounds fully loaded. To an old 1911-shooter, that's pretty nifty. And it has an all-steel frame. So there, plastic-shooters, nyaaaahh!
