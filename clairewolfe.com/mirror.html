ClaireWolfe.com Mirror

This directory contains a mirror of Claire Wolfe's web site as of 09/26/2007. Claire informed me via email that she was allowing her web hosting to expire, and asked if I was interested in preserving her legacy. I, of course jumped at the chance, and quickly let wget do its thing.

ClaireWolfe.com used to be Claire's web site. The domain expires in March of 2009, but no longer points anywhere. Maybe I'll buy it, if Claire agrees.

The Claire Files is an archive of Claire's writings maintained by Debra Ricketts.
