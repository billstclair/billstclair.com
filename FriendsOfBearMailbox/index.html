Friends of Bear Mailbox

I posted a while back some pictures of a Bear Mailbox that I see on the side of the road on my drive to work. Last week, a space to the right of the bear was cleared and a post was put up. Over the weekend, the wooden figures below appeared. I took these pictures on the morning and evening of 22 August, 2001. Click on any image for a larger version. Some go to HTML files that scale the large JPEG down by a factor of two so it fits a 1024x768 pixel monitor. Click on that picture to get the full-sized image.
