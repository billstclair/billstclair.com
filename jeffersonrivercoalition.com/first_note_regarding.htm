First note regarding this site's status -

This note is from Elias Alias, one of the co-founders of The Jefferson River Coalition.

Thank you for being here, and congratulations for making your way here. I, the Staff, and the Editors of The Jefferson River Coalition welcome you. Information on how to join and / or support the coalition is available here under our heading "The Jefferson River Coalition".

 This site is incomplete, and some articles posted here are incomplete. The site is updated and edited several times per week.

One purpose of this site is to harbor and archive important resources, reference sources, articles, and documents. This site shall be a concentration of research materials. Lots of dry old stuff. This site is for readers, for those who want to discover the hidden history of America, for those who oppose the North American Union and the New World Order's "one-world government". This site is for all who value individual liberty, who wish to live in freedom as the Founders outlined through the U.S. Constitution and the Bill of Rights. This site is for everyone who suspects that there is something terribly wrong in America today. 

Our purpose? To exercise our patriotic duties as Americans and as Montanans and as your good neighbors. We are here to expose criminals who have made their way into our government, both past and present, and have created "governmental criminality". The Jefferson River Coalition is here to assist in the networking of the willing who are devoted to protecting America against criminality emanating from the seats of the People's lawful government, the Federal government of the United States of America. And we are here because we think we have something to say, something which shall prove to be helpful in America's struggle against "enemies from within".

Don Doig and I, Elias Alias, are the primary collectors and publishers of the information you'll find at this website. We're both working men, trying to survive financially in a troubled economic world just like everyone else, and therefore we are not able to create an impressive web site which could compete with the finest sites on the Web. We are working as fast as we can because we sense that time is short in the struggle for freedom. We hope to get enough information published here as quickly as possible. Thank you for your patience and your understanding, and most of all, we thank you for your generous support in all the ways we've received public support thus far.

Thank you,

Elias
