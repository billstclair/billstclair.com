Why are criminals without victims not criminals? Why do courts lack jurisdiction without a victim?

Governments exist for only one reason; to protect and maintain individual rights. Therefore, courts are established by governments to protect and maintain individual rights. Therefore, for a court's jurisdiction to be invoked it has to be invoked to protect and maintain individual rights and can be invoked for no other reason. Therefore, it doesn't take a $100,000 law degree to understand that for the a court's jurisdiction to be invoked, an individual right has to have been violated. Courts hate it when you bring up this sticky little point.

It's called standing. Without standing, the court has no jurisdiction and the case has to be dismissed. The judge has no discretion on this issue. Without standing, the judge must dismiss. Yet, as I said, all judges and prosecutors are criminals and they will violate the law if you let them get away with it. Not letting them get away with it is the key.

To have Standing requires the existance of Corpus Delicti; the body of a crime, also known as the elements of a crime. Corpus Delicti, regardless of where you are, always consists of two elements: 1) Violation of an individual right 2) Fact of loss, injury or harm. Without Corpus Delicti, the plaintiff has no standing and the court's jurisdiction is not invoked and the case must be dismissed. I know this sounds a little repetative, but as a society, we've been programmed to believe that the government can make up any crime they want. This couldn't be further from the truth.

Without the violation of an individual right, the court's jurisdiction can't be invoked. Victimless crime is in fact a nonsensical term. There can be no crime without a victim. The reason for this is that if the government is going to penalize you for not obeying their seatbelt laws, the prosecutor has to have standing to invoke the court's jurisdiction over you. Standing to invoke a court's jurisdiction requires that the plaintiff, the government, proves that the defendant has violated their personal rights and that a personal injury has resulted. There is no way that the police officer or prosecutor can prove that you have violated their individual rights; that they have been personally harmed in some way by your actions. The government can never demonstrate that they have suffered an injury as the result of their rights being violated by the defendant.
