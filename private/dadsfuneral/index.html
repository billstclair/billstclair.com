Col. William W. St. Clair, 1929-2001

This page is dedicated to my Dad, Col. William W. St. Clair. He was born on August 26, 1929 and died on December 3, 2001. His funeral was on December 6, 2001 at the First Presbyterian Church in Cheyenne, Wyoming. He was interred in Beth El cemetary in the military section with full military honors: 3 gun volleys and taps.
