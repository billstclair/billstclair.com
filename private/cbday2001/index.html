Christopher's Birthday 2001

Christopher's was born on January 15, 1992. We celebrated this year on the Saturday before, the thirteenth, at Berkshire West, a health club to which Karla belongs (I lift weights closer to work). Christopher invited 7 friends & his sister. We had pizza, games, cake & ice cream, & presents in the room where I took all these pictures, then swimming for an hour and a half. A good time was had by all, well, maybe "wild" would be a better adjective to describe the crowd. Click on any picture for a larger version.
