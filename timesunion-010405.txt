Apologize? Why?
I don't get it. A Chinese supersonic F-8 fighter flies into an American four engine turboprop, and the Chinese are demanding that _we_ apologize. That's like a hare running into a tortoise and insisting that the tortoise is at fault. It's too bad that the Chinese pilot paid with his life for his bad flying, but he killed himself. The slow plane our guys were flying could not have caused the collision.
