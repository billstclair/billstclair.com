The Twenty-Third Psalm

The Twenty-Third Psalm, from Bobby McFerrin's album, Medicine Music, is one beautiful choir piece. His version is him alone, multi-tracked, but when I heard it played at a Universal Worship, I knew it would be wonderful for our choir. So I started up Finale Notepad, and transcribed it. It's a pretty easy piece for any reasonably good choir.

Embedded below is a YouTube video of the song, with a nice slide-show of Bobby McFerrin photos.

Here's my transcription, in two different keys. The original is in B-flat. I transposed it up a step for our choir, to make the lowest bass note a G instead of an F:
