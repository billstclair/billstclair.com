Dear Bill:


Thank you for contacting me regarding H.R.2987, the Methamphetamine Anti-Proliferation Act of 1999. Please be assured that I understand your concerns.

As you may be aware, I am a co-sponsor of this important legislation. I believe that H.R. 2987, if enacted, will have a positive effect on the citizens of our nation. Provisions to ensure sentencing guidelines for offenses reflect their henious nature and reinforce the need for agressive law enforcement law enforcement are important steps in helping our nation's war against drugs. Additionally, I support the amendment to the Federal code prohibiting and setting penalties for teaching or demonstrating the manufacture of a controlled substances.

I am a strong proponent of free speech and would not support a bill that limits free speech in any way. The aforementioned provision, and others in the bill similar to it, are not limitations on speech, rather, they are actions necessary to prevent further drug related harm to the citizens of our nation.

Thank you for sharing your concerns with me. Please do not hesitate to contact me in the future regarding any questions that may arise.
