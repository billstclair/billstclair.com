Gloryroad: The Writings of George Potter

George Potter writes short stories, and poems, and songs. Plinker-MS lovingly collected, in this Clairefiles thread, links to his work. I saved them here, with George's permission. Enjoy!

According to posts on his Facebook page, George died from a heart attack on or around March 7, 2014. Rest in peace, brother.
