More club posters

These are some of the old Waterman Auction posters. The audacious auction is described on one of the other club pages. The really colorful posters, not among these, from the outrageous poster coloring parties, are in private collections. Most of the designs were made by Robert Grunditz, a famous Fairbanks artist sort. The faces on one of the posters are local and visiting climber sorts. If anyone remembers the last name of Brad or Brandon from Colorado, and the name of his girl friend, let us know.
