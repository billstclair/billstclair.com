The Holocaust. Here is a photograph of a German policeman finishing off Jewish women who remain alive in a ravine south of Rovno in Ukraine. These women had been taken from the Mizocz ghetto on its liquidation, forced to strip, and shot en mass. Documentation: see Democide. Source: This is #17878 in the collection of Holocaust photographs displayed by the United States Memorial Museum.
