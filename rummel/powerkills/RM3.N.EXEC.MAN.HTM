The Nazi's murdered about 21,000,000 people, among whom were near 5,3000,000 Jews in what is now called the Holocaust. Photos of Nazi victims, therefore, cannot automatically be assumed to be Jews, although this is often taken for granted, and often appropriately so, as in the case of this photo of a man awaiting death at the edge of a pit filled with the mass murdered.
