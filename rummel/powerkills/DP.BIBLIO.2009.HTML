Democratic Peace Bibliography 

Version August, 2009

 

NOTE

This is a bibliography of the democratic peace narrowly defined as democracies don’t make war on each other, or are most peaceful internally. It does not include works on democracy or war in themselves. Moreover, it is most thorough for the years up to 2000-2009. For version 3.0 that covers the years up to 2000, see here. Bibliographic formats are mixed.

 

Please help on this. I am trying to make this as comprehensive as possible and error free. If you see an error in an author’s name or title, please let me know. Similarly, if I miss a speech, paper, article, or book on the democratic peace, please inform me. Contact email: Rummel is at Hawaii.edu.
