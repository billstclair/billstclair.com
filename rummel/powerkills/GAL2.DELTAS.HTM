Paintage. Delta was a very loving pet cat of ours. I came across a photo I had taken of her many years ago and was struck by the smooth curve of her body, from the top of her tail to front foreleg. Because of this I tried to make a paintage, composing clones and their painted shadows into a complementary S-shape, one of the more beautiful natural curves in nature. The resulting composition is pleasing to my eye.
