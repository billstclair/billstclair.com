Germans are about to murder this old lady in the Belzec concentration camp. Documentation: Democide: Nazi Genocide and Mass Murder. Source: "The Undeniable Holocaust." From Prof. Leopold Pfefferberg-Page Collection, via the Main Commission for the Investigation of Nazi War Crimes, Warsaw, Poland.

I was so depressed by this photo-night after night I kept seeing it--that I finally tried to make a painting of how I saw it--this poor old lady shivering in the snow alone, no friends or love ones around to give her support and courage, waiting to be murdered.
