Tennis Anyone? Kaneohe, Hawaii. Paintage: watercolor, pastel, airbrush. For exercise I walk, and play tennis (old people's doubles) with my wife Grace early in the morning on the court you see in this painting. This is a typical early tennis morning scene, with the rising sun turning the Koolau mountains to gold. Of course, its the distraction of such beauty that causes my high number of unforced errors.
