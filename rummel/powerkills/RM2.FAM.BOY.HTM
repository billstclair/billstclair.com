The fundamentalist Arab, Muslim military government of Sudan has been waging a war against their black and non-Muslim south. Perhaps 2,000,000 have been killed in the war and resulting famine, much of which has been purposely caused by government destruction of food sources, including the bombing of wells and relief supplies. Here is shown what I believe is a starving boy in the South whose life expectancy, given his condition, must now be short For a description of this civil war, see Chapter 1 of Saving Lives, Enriching Life.
