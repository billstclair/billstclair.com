Arrowverse

Introduction

The CW Network is known for its DC super-hero shows. Netflix recently acquired rights to some of the finished shows. I’ve been bingeing Supergirl, The Flash, Arrow, and DC’s Legends of Tomorrow. I noticed some cross-over while watching Supergirl. The cross-over between Arrow, and The Flash is frequent; characters from one city visit the other. Many of Legends of Tomorrow’s characters appear in Arrow or The Flash. I created this page after noticing those cross-overs. I decided to finish my binge by watching the shows in the order they were originally aired.

There’s an Arrowverse Wiki on Fandom and an Arrowverse page on Wikipedia, which has a table of Official crossover events.
