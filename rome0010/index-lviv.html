Lviv/Rome, October 2000

This page is the story and pictures of my trip to Lviv, Ukraine and Rome, Italy on 20-30 October, 2000. I travelled with my boss, Maynard and my coworker, Viktor. We flew from Albany to JFK in New York City to We went to Rome from Lviv, Ukraine, where we were visiting on business. We arrived on Friday afternoon, Octber 27, spent all day Saturday and Sunday touring Rome, and flew back to Albany, NY on Monday, October 30.
