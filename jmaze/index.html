Click on the image to enable keyboard control. Use the arrow keys to move around the maze. Press the space bar to shoot a missile.

There are three robots. Bob is a slow simple robot who goes straight until he runs into a wall and then prefers to turn left. Bill is a smarter and faster robot. Be careful of him. Joe is a drunk. Ignore him. Bill and Bob will shoot you. Joe is unarmed. If two missiles collide, they are both destroyed. You can only have one missile in the air at a time.

Though Joe moves around under his own "volition", he is not identified to the game as a robot. True robots do not shoot each other, but Bob and Bill will happily shoot Joe.

You cannot move through another player unless that player is a ghost, identified by "(ghost)" after his name. Bob is a ghost. The other players are not.

Network play, better artwork, an active overhead view, and lots more coming soon. Have fun!
