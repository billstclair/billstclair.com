ABOUT THE AUTHOR:

FULTON HUXTABLE

Fulton Huxtable currently resides in south Florida and works for a major corporation in the area.

Previously, from 1971 to 2000, he was president of Huxtable Associates, an executive search firm based in St. Charles (St. Louis), Missouri. 

Numerous articles by him have appeared, over the years, in various publications such as Barron's, The Freeman, Indiana Business, Personnel Administrator, Administrative Management, The Free Radical, WorldNetDaily and others, promoting a philosophy of rationality, individualism and liberty for all.   He received his undergraduate degree in English from Arkansas State University.   He earned his Master's degree in political science from Purdue University in 1970.   While at Purdue he taught courses in political philosophy.
