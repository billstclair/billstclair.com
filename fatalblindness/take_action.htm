Current legislative status (10-18-99):  In August of 1998, Shays-Meehan passed the House by 252-179.  In the Senate, 52 Senators supported it but the bill died because there were not 60 votes to close debate and bring the bill to the floor for a final vote.  A new version of Shays-Meehan passed the House in the summer of 1999 and is now before the Senate in the form of the McCain-Feingold bill.    Proponents of McCain-Feingold, on October 18, 1999, failed to garner enough votes to force a vote on the bill.   This has bought us some time to eventually have the bill killed altogether.
