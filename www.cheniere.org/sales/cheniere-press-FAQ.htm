Building An Overunity Device

Q:  I want to build my own MEG, experimental unit, overunity device, free energy machine... 
Where can I get a schematic?  Do you offer a kit of parts?  Does the book tell how I can build my own?

A:  We do not currently sell a "kit of parts".  Tom Bearden has explained why many times.  Read his answer...  Energy from the Vacuum  is a textbook for the new physics, not an experimenter's handbook.  Energy from the Vacuum is probably necessary, but not sufficient, for building a working prototype unit.  Tom Bearden is a theoretician who explains the scientific principles involved, not a hands-on "bench person".

Free Energy Generation: Circuits & Schematics by John Bedini and Tom Bearden and Part 2 in the Energy from the Vacuum DVD series can help experiments and prototype builders get started. 

Cheniere Press

Q:  What does the Cheniere Press logo represent?

A:  The Cheniere Press logo passing the baton symbolizes the transfer of vital knowledge to a new generation of scientists, engineers and researchers for the betterment of the human condition. Tom Bearden has been running this foot-race alone for many decades.  At a defining moment in history, we think it's about time he got some help!

Q:  Who is "Cheniere Press"?

A:  Cheniere Press is a small team of volunteers dedicated to disseminating information about Tom Bearden's work, believing it holds extraordinary promise for our world and for humanity.  The volunteers are professionals and retirees from several professional fields, who give up their precious free time to make sure this information is published and to assist Tom Bearden and John Bedini with their projects.

About DVDs

Q.  What do you mean by "NTSC" and "PAL" video?

A.  They are standards for analog video signals as used in different parts of the world.  

  • Countries that use NTSC include:  U.S.A., Canada, Mexico, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Venezuela, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan.

  • Countries that use  PAL include:  U.K., Argentina, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Denmark, Finland, Germany, Holland, Hong Kong, India, Ireland, Israel, Italy, New Zealand, Norway, Portugal, Romania, Singapore, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey.

  • Countries that use (or have used) SÉCAM include:  France, Greece, Luxembourg, Romania, Russia.  We do not offer DVDs in the SÉCAM format at this time.

The DVD drives in modern computers generally can play DVDs in either format and you would not notice any difference.  The format is more important if you plan to view the DVD on a television set.

Q:  What is your policy on replacing defective or unplayable DVDs?

A:  Our manufacturing process includes automated validation against the master to ensure DVD quality.  We will replace DVDs that are defective or damaged, but not ones that you cannot play because the type you requested is not supported by your player.  In February, 2006 we switched  from the DVD+R ("plus-R")  format to the DVD-R ("minus-R") format, which seems to be compatible with the majority of DVD players.  Before ordering, ensure that you know what format your player can play.  If you are uncertain, then please do not order a DVD. Sorry, No DVD refunds. If you are having trouble with a DVD purchased from Cheniere Press, please read our DVD Diagnostics page before contacting us, as it may help solve the problem.

About PayPal

Q:  What is PayPal?

A:  PayPal is the world's #1 online payment service.  We use PayPal as our credit card and electronic funds transfer acceptance service.  PayPal handles all the details of verifying your payment information in a secure online environment; we never see your private data.  You don't need a PayPal account to make a payment with PayPal. You can pay from a credit card or bank account.  For further information, see https://www.paypal.com. 

Q:  How do I use the Shopping Cart? ("Add to Cart" button)

A:  The shopping cart is available throughout our website.  Anytime you see an Add to Cart button, you can add that item to your shopping cart.  The shopping cart remembers your selections until you pay for them.  When you are done shopping and want to pay for your order, click the View Cart button on any web page.  You will be prompted for your shipping address and payment method (credit card or bank account) in a secure environment provided by PayPal.  Over 56 million people in 45 countries use PayPal, the world's #1 online payment service.  If you cannot or do not wish to use PayPal, we accept checks and money orders with the printable order form.

Q:  I can't see any Add to Cart buttons!  What are you talking about?

A:  Recently, some Web browsers and/or software firewalls started blocking the display of PayPal buttons.  We are looking for a technical solution to this problem.  Meanwhile, we suggest two workarounds:  (1)  Turn off your Ad Blocker temporarily.  (2)  Try a different browser. 

Q:  PayPal is not available for residents of my country.   
      I do not feel comfortable using PayPal.  
      I do not use credit cards.
      Something goes wrong when I try to use PayPal.

A:  No problem.  You can mail a check or money order to pay for your order.  The Printable Order Form tells where to mail your payment.

Q:  Why must I use PayPal?  Why can't you just take my credit card number?

A:  We do not offer credit card pay-by-phone service. If you want to use a credit card, the only way we handle that is via our website, which uses PayPal's credit card authorization service as well as its electronic shopping cart function.  There is no fee when you use PayPal to make a payment.  You do not have to establish an account with PayPal in order to make a payment facilitated by PayPal.  If there is a problem with your PayPal order, we can issue a credit. 

Q:  In my country, the normal procedure is to wire money directly from my bank or post office to your bank account.  I don't see your bank-wire routing information on the website. 

A:  For security, we do not publish information about Cheniere Press's bank account.  Thus, we do not accept electronic payments directly.  If you want to fund your payment from a bank account, you can do so through PayPal. 

Q:  Solutions to common PayPal issues.

1.  The shopping cart does not work.  Items disappeared from my cart.  I can't navigate past the first screen.  I don't see where to provide my shipping address. 

A:  These symptoms appear if your web browser does not have "cookies" enabled.  Please refer to your web browser's Help to learn how to enable session cookies.  (A firewall is another place where your computer might be blocking cookies.)  A cookie is a small piece of data exchanged between your computer and the website.  It helps navigation by  remembering the state of your online session.  If your browser blocks session cookies, then PayPal's server cannot remember what's in your shopping cart or data that you input on the previous screen.

2.  Can you ship to a different address than my billing address?

A:  Yes, at the present time we allow you to input any shipping address.  Feel free to write in comments to help us ship your items to the right address.

3.  PayPal will not take my credit card. 

A:  It might happen that you entered a credit card number some time in the past, and PayPal remembers your old credit card, which has now expired.  You try to input the same credit card number with a new expiration date but you can't.  The solution is delete your expired credit card number.  Then PayPal will prompt you to enter a new credit card number. You can now enter the same credit card number with the new expiration date.

Q:  I cannot use PayPal.  I do not want to use PayPal. 

A:  If you cannot or do not want to use PayPal, we accept checks and money orders by postal mail. See our Printable Order Form for further information.

Privacy Policy

Q:  What is your Privacy Policy?

A:  We automatically enroll anyone who buys a product from Cheniere Press in the Cheniere Announcements list, which will notify you of website updates and new Cheniere products.  You can manage your subscription by emailing us and you can opt out at any time.

Our website visitor information is private.  We take the same steps to protect your personal information that we take to protect our own.  If you are concerned about the security or  privacy of any piece of personal information, then do not send it to us.  Cheniere.org does not use cookies.  Like most web sites, our web server collects data on which web pages are accessed (hit counts), and logs the home domain (ISP) of clients accessing our site.  This helps us improve website organization, tune the website to make it more accessible, and understand which material is most popular.

Q:  Can I telephone you about my order?

A:  No, we feel strongly about our privacy too, so our telephone number is unlisted.  We prefer that you email us at Cheniere Press; we usually reply the same day.  As a web- based business with customers in time zones worldwide, it is infeasible for us to staff a 24-hour telephone line.
