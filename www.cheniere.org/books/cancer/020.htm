DEFINITIONS (CONTINUED)

  • POTENTIAL - any ordering, either static or dynamic
    or combination, in the VPF of vacuum
    - same definition as energy
    - must have internal order, hence structure

  • SCALAR POTENTIAL - any static ordering in the VPF
    of vacuum, with respect of the external observer

  • VECTOR POTENTIAL - any dynamic (non-stationary)
    ordering in the VPF of vacuum.
    - must have internal order, hence structure
