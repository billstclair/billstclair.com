PRESENT DEFINITION OF "ENERGY" IS ERRONEOUS

  • PRESENT:  "ENERGY IS THE CAPACITY TO DO WORK."

  • ACTUALLY, WORK IS THE SCATTERING (DISSIPATION)
    OF ENERGY.

  • THE PRESENT DEFINITION STATES THAT "ENERGY IS
    THE CAPACITY TO DO SCATTERING OF ENERGY."

  • ENERGY CAN BE SCATTERED, YIELDING WORK.  BUT
    THE CAPACITY TO PERFORM THAT SCATTERING IS
    NOT ENERGY!  (E.G., IT MAY BE RESISTANCE.)

  • COMPARE TO:  "A FISHHOOK IS THE CAPACITY TO
    CATCH FISH."

  • ACTUALLY, A FISHHOOK IS A BENT WIRE WITH A 
    BARB.  IT HAS THE CAPACITY TO CATCH FISH (GIVEN
    OTHER CONDITIONS).  BUT THE CAPACITY TO CATCH
    FISH IS NOT WHAT IT IS.
