SOME PROBLEMS IN FOUNDATIONS
OF QUANTUM MECHANICS

  • EXCLUDES CHAOS (HIDDEN ORDER), AND THIS
    IS KNOWN TO BE IN ERROR

  • ORDERED MACROREALITY CANNOT EMERGE FROM
    INTEGRATING DISORDERED (RANDOM) CHANGES

  • USES RANDOM-VARIABLE STATISTICS FROM
    GIBBS THERMODYNAMICS

  • NOTION THAT QUANTUM CHANGE - HENCE ROOTS
    OF PHYSICAL REALITY - CANNOT BE ENGINEERED

  • REQUIRES ACTION-AT-A-DISTANCE EFFECTS

  • CONTRADICTS CLASSICAL MECHANICS, GENERAL
    RELATIVITY, AND CLASSICAL ELECTROMAGNETICS
