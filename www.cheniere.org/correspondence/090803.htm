During World War II, Nazi Germany modified, perfected, and used a U.S. process for manufacturing clean-burning synthetic fuel from coal.  This "Holy Grail" was one of the secrets re-imported into a few select hands after the War.

It is also a matter of public record that during the latter part of the last century, several cartels bought up all the coal in the U.S.  It has been postulated that this was to await the day when oil would be on the wane, and they could apply this secret process to their vast coal holdings to produce synthetic fuel.

The foregoing is fact, not fiction, and formed the basis of the 1980s film "The Formula", starring George C Scott and Marlon Brando (I have a copy - they are hard to get - I wonder why?).
