Excerpt from Private Correspondence

Many years ago (I mean WAY back there!), I also met Professor Laithwaite in England – that is how I found out about the Heaviside component of energy flow that had been suppressed. Laithwaite knew about it, and believed gyros could take some energy from that curled component, and he gave me the citations. I checked them out at RSIC, and there it all was! Laithwaite is dead now, but one of the things he did was to develop a little no-battery, solid state, contraption in which a ball just continuously rolled around, without ceasing. He delivered this demonstration unit to some technical museum in the UK, and when I get a chance I’m going to see if I can find out what happened to it, or more information on it. It definitely is one of the things that also has been very quietly suppressed.
