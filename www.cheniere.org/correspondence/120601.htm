Eventually, yes, but not right now.  We hope to get the first commercial unit ready for production about a year from now, at about 2.5 KW.  Also, we plan it to be modular, so one can "gang" groups of them.  And develop the synchronizer simultaneously.  Hence 4 such units ganged and synchronized would give a 10 KW unit.  For the second generation (perhaps a year later), we hope to have a basic 10 KW unit, also modular.  At that time, with ganged four of them we could have a modular 40 KW unit available.

 

But there's still a year's very hard work before we are ready to market a unit at all.  The first ones will be placed on the market in a foreign country, where the research is now being done.  That should be about a year from now.
