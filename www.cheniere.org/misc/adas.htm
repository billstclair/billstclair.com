The Association of Distinguished American Scientists is a tiny, tax-exempt, non-profit 501c(3) foundation incorporated in the State of Washington.  It was founded in the 1960s by Dr. John Hubbard, meteorologist for the original atomic bomb test team, and other atomic scientists from that team.  From its inception until his death in 1992, Dr. Hubbard was the President of the Association.  Upon Dr. Hubbard's untimely passing, LTC (Retired) T.E. Bearden was elected President, and together with the Board moved the archives of the Association to Huntsville, Alabama. 

The membership of the Association is restricted to those scientists and inventors who are awarded the Distinguished Scientist Award by the Association. 

Our present efforts are focused toward unification of the major disciplines of science, namely classical EM (CEM), general relativity (GR), and quantum mechanics (QM).  Since these three main disciplines are presently inconsistent with each other, our approach has been to examine the foundations concepts and postulates of each of the three.  We believe we have uncovered major flaws in their foundations, and that these flaws—particularly in the foundations of CEM—have heretofore prevented (1) successful extraction of electrical energy from the vacuum, (2) engineerable antigravity, (3) an extension of EM to provide a basis for developing therapeutic treatment and cures of dread diseases such as cancer and AIDS, along the lines pioneered by Prioré, and (4) an engineerable, testable theory of mind, thought, and deep cellular control in living systems.  In short, we believe we have uncovered an approach not just to extended, unified EM/GR/QM, but also to a testable unified theory of mind and matter and their interaction. 

For some years ADAS exerted effort to uncover the basis for secret Soviet weapons development in the extended CEM area.  That effort reached a highpoint in the mid 1990s, and has continued at a lower level since then.  We intensified our efforts with regards to "free" energy (from a hidden, "free" source, the quantum mechanical vacuum), antigravity, and extended time-polarized EM wave healing foundations.  We are trying to establish a firm basis for these three areas, and we believe we have made slow but steady progress. 

The Foundation is very minimally funded each year by small donations from a few benefactors.  No officer or director of the Association draws any salary whatsoever.  All funds received are disbursed directly on official projects, and at least 85% or more of the funds received are expended in the same year received. 

A major part of our effort is the preparation of scientific papers for distribution by the Association, and papers for presentation at scientific and technical symposia and workshops.  We have participated in producing the first audited unconventional energy data base in existence (under auspices of the Planetary Association for Clean Energy (PACE) in Ottawa, Canada—which was in turn under contract to the Canadian Government.  In addition, our President has worked with, or maintained a relationship with, several inventors and many scientists in the overunity energy area.
