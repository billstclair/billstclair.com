The Motionless Electromagnetic Generator - MEG

In the year 2000, a team headed by Dr. Tom Bearden successfully tested an Overunity device known as the Motionless Electromagnetic Generator.

Consisting of a modified pulsed transformer utilizing the Bohm-Aharonov Effect, the overunity experiments have been replicated by researcher J-L Naudin in France.

Further details will be released when Patent matters have been concluded.

An explanation of its functioning appeared in Foundations of Physics Letters, Vol. 14, No. 1, 2001 - "Explanation of the Motionless Electromagnetic Generator with O(3) Electrodynamics, by 14 authors.
